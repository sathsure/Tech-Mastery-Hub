## How to use this list

- These are candidate canonical topics, not newly invented learning content.
- Existing source material remains authoritative until reviewed.
- A topic should become a canonical concept when it represents reusable knowledge referenced by roadmap, interview, practice, visual-note, masterclass, or project material.
- Do not duplicate entire source documents into concept files.
- Concept pages should become the stable knowledge layer that other repository areas can reference.
