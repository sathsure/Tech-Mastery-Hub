# Salary, Payslips & Compensation (India)

## Purpose

Understand an Indian offer letter and payslip: how CTC becomes gross salary and then take-home pay, what each allowance and deduction means, how income tax is worked out under the old and new regimes, and how bonuses, ESOPs and RSUs are paid and taxed. Figures are for FY / Tax Year 2026-27.

## Core Topics

### Salary structure
- CTC, fixed and variable pay, gross and net salary, LPA, total compensation
- Basic salary, employer cost, employer and employee PF, gratuity
- Performance, joining and retention bonuses, ESOPs and RSUs

### Allowances
- HRA, DA, special, conveyance, transport, medical, LTA, meal, phone and internet
- Which are taxable, exempt or partly exempt in each regime

### Payslip
- Earnings, deductions and net pay
- Employee PF, professional tax, TDS, ESI, LOP

### Tax
- Old vs new regime, slabs, standard deduction, 87A rebate
- Deductions (80C, 80D, NPS), taxable income, TDS certificate

### CTC to take-home
- Worked examples for ₹10, ₹15, ₹20 and ₹25 LPA
- Offer letter checks and HR terms
