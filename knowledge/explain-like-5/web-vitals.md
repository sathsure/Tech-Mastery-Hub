# Web Vitals & Performance — Explain Like I'm 5

> Hard ideas as everyday pictures, then what they really mean.

## LCP
🍽️ How long until the main dish arrives at your table — not just the water.
**Really:** time until the biggest visible content is shown.

## INP
🔔 You press the doorbell — how long before someone opens the door?
**Really:** how quickly the screen responds after you click, tap or type.

## CLS
📖 You're reading and someone keeps moving the book, so you lose your line.
**Really:** how much the page jumps around while loading.

## Lazy loading
🍿 The cinema brings snacks only when you reach your seat, not all at the entrance.
**Really:** load images and code only when they're needed.

## CDN
🏪 Small shops in every neighbourhood instead of one big shop far away.
**Really:** files are served from servers near the user.

## Long tasks
🚧 One lorry blocking a single-lane road; everyone behind it waits.
**Really:** long JavaScript work blocks the page from responding.
