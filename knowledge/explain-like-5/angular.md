# Angular — Explain Like I'm 5

> Hard ideas as everyday pictures, then what they really mean.

## Components
🧱 LEGO bricks: small pieces you snap together to build a castle.
**Really:** reusable UI pieces combined into a page.

## Dependency injection
🍱 At school, lunch is delivered to your desk — you don't cook it yourself.
**Really:** Angular creates services and hands them to the classes that need them.

## Change detection
👀 A teacher walks around the class checking every student's notebook after any noise.
**Really:** Angular checks components and updates the screen when something happens.

## OnPush
🙋 The teacher only checks students who raise their hands.
**Really:** a component is checked only when its inputs or signals change, or it handles an event.

## Signals
💡 A light switch wired to specific bulbs: flip it and only those bulbs change.
**Really:** reactive values that update exactly the parts that use them.

## Observables
🚰 A tap: water flows over time, and you can turn it off.
**Really:** streams of values you subscribe to and unsubscribe from.

## Lazy loading
🏬 A shop opens a room only when a customer asks for that section.
**Really:** load a page's code only when the user visits it.
