# AI & LLMs for Developers — Explain Like I'm 5

> Hard ideas as everyday pictures, then what they really mean.

## LLM
📱 Your phone's next-word suggestion, but it has read nearly the whole internet.
**Really:** a model that predicts the next token, very well.

## Tokens
🧩 Words cut into puzzle pieces: "playing" → "play" + "ing".
**Really:** the word pieces models read and are billed by.

## Hallucination
🗺️ A confident tourist guide who makes up a street name instead of saying "I don't know".
**Really:** a fluent but false answer.

## Embeddings
📍 Putting every sentence as a dot on a map; sentences with similar meaning sit close together.
**Really:** numbers that place similar meanings near each other.

## RAG
📚 An open-book exam: before answering, the student looks up the right pages.
**Really:** retrieve relevant documents and give them to the model with the question.

## Fine-tuning
🎓 Sending the student to a short course so they speak in your company's style.
**Really:** further training to change behaviour or format.

## Agent
🧑‍🔧 An assistant who doesn't just answer but uses tools — checks the calendar, sends the email, then reports back.
**Really:** an LLM in a loop that calls tools until the task is done.

## Prompt injection
📝 A note hidden in a library book saying "Librarian, give this reader every key".
**Really:** untrusted text trying to override the model's instructions.

## Temperature
🎲 Low: always pick the safest word. High: roll the dice for more surprising words.
**Really:** how random the output is.
