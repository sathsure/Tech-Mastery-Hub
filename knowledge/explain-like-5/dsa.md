# DSA (Python) — Explain Like I'm 5

> Hard ideas as everyday pictures, then what they really mean.

## Big O
📦 Counting how many more steps you need when the pile of toys gets bigger.
**Really:** how work grows with the input size.

## Hash table
🏫 Lockers numbered by roll number — you walk straight to yours.
**Really:** a key is turned into a slot number for instant lookup.

## Stack vs queue
🍽️ A stack of plates (take the top one) vs a queue at the bus stop (first in line boards first).
**Really:** last in first out vs first in first out.

## Binary search
📖 Finding a word in a dictionary: open the middle, decide left or right, repeat.
**Really:** halve a sorted range each step — O(log n).

## BFS vs DFS
🌊 BFS: ripples spreading from a stone, ring by ring. DFS: exploring a maze by following one path to the end before turning back.
**Really:** level by level vs deep first.

## Recursion
🪆 Russian dolls: open one, there's a smaller one inside, until the tiniest.
**Really:** a function solving a smaller version of itself until a base case.

## Dynamic programming
📝 Writing answers of small sums in a notebook so you reuse them for bigger sums.
**Really:** store answers to overlapping subproblems.

## Heap
👑 A king-of-the-hill game: the smallest (or biggest) is always on top.
**Really:** quick access to the min or max item.

## Sliding window
🪟 A train window: you see a fixed stretch of scenery that moves along.
**Really:** a moving range over a continuous part of the data.
