# Git — Explain Like I'm 5

> Hard ideas as everyday pictures, then what they really mean.

## Commit
📸 Taking a photo of your LEGO model so you can always go back to how it looked.
**Really:** a saved snapshot of your files.

## Staging area
🛒 The shopping basket — you choose what goes in before paying (committing).
**Really:** where you pick which changes go into the next commit.

## Branch
🌿 A copy of your drawing where you try new colours without spoiling the original.
**Really:** a separate line of work, just a pointer to a commit.

## Merge vs rebase
🧶 Merge ties two ropes together with a knot. Rebase unties your rope and re-ties it at the end of the other, so it looks like one straight rope.
**Really:** combine histories with a merge commit vs replay your commits on top.

## Merge conflict
✍️ Two people edited the same sentence differently — someone must decide which wins.
**Really:** Git can't combine changes to the same lines automatically.

## Stash
👜 Shoving your unfinished homework in a drawer to clear the desk, then taking it back later.
**Really:** temporarily parking uncommitted changes.

## Revert vs reset
↩️ Revert: write a new diary entry saying "ignore yesterday". Reset: tear yesterday's page out.
**Really:** undo with a new commit vs rewrite history.
